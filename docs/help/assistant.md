# About the Aliob assistant

The in-app assistant can be reached from the floating chat bubble on any
screen, or from Profile → Help & Support.

## Say "hi" and it already knows the usual reasons people write in

The assistant does not open with a bare hello. It greets you and immediately
offers the things people most often need, so you can pick one instead of
describing your problem from scratch:

- **Verification or a rejection** — your documents, or why an application was
  turned down
- **Appeal or report something** — a dispute, spam, fraud, or a suspension
- **Payment or recharge delayed** — a recharge or order payment that has not
  landed
- **An order or a ride problem** — wrong, missing, late, or a driver issue
- **Your account** — profile, phone number, password, or closing your account
- **Prices and fees** — why a fare differs by vehicle type or by city, and
  what the platform fee covers
- **Finding a provider** — browsing providers and seeing their photos before
  you choose

Pick the closest one and it takes you straight to the right screen, or answers
with the exact steps. If none of them fit, just describe what is going on and
it will work it out — you do not need to classify your own problem first.

**If you are not sure which kind of account you have**, say so. The assistant
will ask you one question — user or provider — and then answer. It asks once
and then gets on with it; it will not walk you through a form.

You can also ask **"what can you do"** at any point for the full list rather
than the common ones.

## What it can do

- **Answer how-to questions** about every part of the app: signing up,
  buying and orders, escrow and payments, wallet recharges, fees, points,
  rides, and provider tools.
- **Explain prices.** Ask it why a fare differs by vehicle type or by city,
  what the platform fee covers, or what a particular booking will cost — it
  explains that fees are set per category and per city, so a three-wheeler and
  a heavy truck are not charged the same, and the rate follows the city the
  trip starts in.
- **Talk you through finding a provider.** Ask how to see a provider's photos,
  where their gallery is, or how to browse providers before choosing. It will
  point you at **All providers** on the home screen and the photos on a
  provider's page.
- **Explain pickup locations.** Ask why the app asks for your area or kebele
  rather than just the city — it drives the driver to the right street, and
  you can always set the pickup point yourself on the map.
- **Check your live account status** when you ask, for example:
  - "What is my wallet balance?"
  - "Where is my order?" / "Track my order"
  - "What's the status of my ride?"
  - "When is my subscription due?"
  - "What happened to my appeal?"
- **Give you shortcut buttons**: most answers include tappable links that
  take you straight to the right screen — *Recharge my wallet*, *My orders*,
  *Book a ride*, *Check my appeals*, and so on.
- **Report a problem**: if you tell it something went wrong with an order, a
  payment, or another party, it collects the details and sends them to the
  support team as a ticket. For disputes, scams, account bans or suspensions,
  and ride or driver issues, it opens the **Submit an appeal** form already
  tagged with the issue type, so the right team context reviews it.

## What it will not pretend to do

Some things need a person. The assistant says so plainly rather than
guessing at an answer:

- **A real bug** in the app — it files it for the team and tells you it has.
- **A dispute needing human judgement** — a disagreement over a payment, a
  delivery, or who was at fault. It opens the appeal form with your details
  rather than ruling on it.
- **Anything sensitive** — a fraud report, a suspension you want reviewed, a
  legal or safety matter. Straight to the appeal form, tagged, with evidence.

It will not invent a resolution, and it will not tell you your case is
settled when a person still has to look at it.

## Checking on something you already filed

Use **Appeal → Check my appeals** in the app (or ask the assistant, which
will open it) to see the status of anything you have submitted and any
resolution recorded against it.

## Privacy

The assistant only ever sees your own account information, never other
people's. It never shares phone numbers or email addresses, and every
follow-up happens inside the app. Your chat messages are stored so the team
can improve the help quality; never share passwords or payment card details
in chat.

## Feedback

Every answer has **Helpful?** buttons. Your thumbs up or down tells the
team which answers need improvement — use them freely.
