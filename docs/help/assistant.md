# About the Aliob assistant

The in-app assistant can be reached from the floating chat bubble on any
screen, or from Profile → Help & Support.

## What it can do

- **Answer how-to questions** about every part of the app: signing up,
  buying and orders, escrow and payments, wallet recharges, fees, points,
  rides, and provider tools.
- **Check your live account status** when you ask, for example:
  - "What is my wallet balance?"
  - "Where is my order?" / "Track my order"
  - "What's the status of my ride?"
  - "When is my subscription due?"
- **Give you shortcut buttons**: many answers include tappable links that
  take you straight to the right screen (for example *Recharge my wallet*
  or *Book a ride*).
- **Report a problem**: if you tell the assistant something went wrong with
  an order, a payment, or another party, it collects the details and sends
  them to the support team as a ticket, then the team follows up by phone.
- **Hand you to a human** whenever it can't help: support WhatsApp, phone,
  and email are shown in the chat.

## Feedback

Every answer has **Helpful?** buttons. Your thumbs up or down tells the
team which answers need improvement — use them freely.

## Privacy

The assistant only ever sees your own account information, never other
people's. Your chat messages are stored so the team can improve the help
quality; never share passwords or payment card details in chat.
