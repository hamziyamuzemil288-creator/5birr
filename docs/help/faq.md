## Creating an account

**How do I sign up?**
Tap "Get Started" on the home screen, choose whether you want a personal
account, a vehicle provider account, or a service provider account, then
fill in your details. You'll need a photo of your national ID. Vehicle
providers also need a photo of their driving license, and service providers
a photo of their trading license.

**Why do I need to upload my ID?**
Every account is verified by our team before it becomes active. This keeps
the marketplace safe and honest for everyone.

**What happens after I register?**
Your application goes to our review team. Most reviews finish quickly —
once you're approved, you can sign in and start right away.

**Why was I asked to correct my information?**
Sometimes a photo is unreadable or details don't match. You'll get a
notification explaining what to fix, and you can resubmit your documents.

**Can I use the same account as both a customer and a provider?**
One account is one role. If you also want to sell or provide rides, register
a provider account with the same details.

## Buying and ordering

**How do I buy something?**
Find a product, open it, and add it to your cart. From the cart you can
adjust quantities, then check out. You can pay by uploading your payment
screenshot — your order is confirmed once the payment is verified.

**What is the payment verification step?**
After you place an order, upload a screenshot of your payment. Our team
checks it, and once it's confirmed the order moves forward and the seller
is notified.

**Can I buy from several sellers at once?**
Yes. Your cart groups items by seller, and each seller's items are checked
out as a separate order, so every seller is paid correctly.

**How do the fees work?**
Orders include a small transaction fee shown clearly at checkout before you
pay. Providers pay a fixed monthly subscription after their account is
approved — there are no hidden charges.

**What does "escrow" mean for my order?**
Your payment is held safely until the order is confirmed. The seller's
contact and live location details are only shared with you after your
payment is confirmed — this protects both sides of every deal.

**How do order numbers work?**
Every order gets a unique number based on its category — **Vh** for
vehicles, **Ht** for household goods, **Fd** for food, and so on (for
example Vh00012). Quote this number whenever you contact support about an
order, and have it ready if you need to report a problem.

## Contacting and meeting sellers

**When can I see the seller's location or contact details?**
After your payment is confirmed, the order screen shows a "share live
location" option and directions to the meeting point. Before that, details
stay private on purpose.

**How do I find sellers near me?**
The search screen sorts results by distance from where you are. Allow
location access when the app asks, and nearby results will show how far
away each seller is.

**Why does the app ask for my location?**
Only to show you nearby sellers and to help your driver navigate to you
during a ride. You can decline and still use the app, but distances to
sellers will not be shown.

## Rides and vehicle providers

**How do I book a ride?**
Choose your pickup point on the ride screen — the app fills it in
automatically if you allow location access — and a nearby driver will
accept your request.

**Can I see my driver's location?**
Yes. Once a driver claims your ride, you can watch their vehicle move on
the map in real time and tap "Get directions" to navigate to them.

**Can a driver see where I am?**
Drivers see your pickup point when they accept the ride, and you can choose
to share your live location so they can navigate to you. Location sharing
between you and the driver is only available for that ride.

## Selling as a provider

**How do I add a product for sale?**
From your provider dashboard, open the product form, add photos, set your
price and stock, and publish. Products appear in search right away.

**How do I get paid?**
Every approved provider gets a wallet. Confirmed sales are recorded there,
minus the small transaction fee, and the monthly subscription is deducted
automatically when it's due.

**How do I recharge my wallet?**
Open **Wallet** from your provider dashboard and tap **RECHARGE WALLET**.
Send the amount to our Telebirr account **0917686990** (Aliob Business) —
the account details are shown right on the recharge screen. Then enter the
amount, the transaction reference, and upload your payment screenshot. Once
our team verifies the payment, your wallet is credited. You can track the
status of every recharge request on the wallet screen.

**What is the one-time registration fee?**
After your provider account is approved, one step remains: pay a one-time
**100 birr registration fee** with your first wallet recharge. Until then
you can't upload listings or receive orders. Your first recharge must be
**at least 100 birr** — the fee activates your account and anything above
it is credited to your wallet. Later recharges are credited in full.

**Why can't I upload a listing or accept orders?**
Almost always because the one-time registration fee hasn't been paid yet —
see the recharge section above. Orders also pause whenever your wallet
balance is 0, because fees are deducted from the wallet. Recharge and
you're fully back.

**What if my wallet balance is too low?**
You'll see a low-balance warning on your wallet, and the app notifies you
when a fee can't be covered. Just recharge and you're back in business —
recharging is the only action needed on your side.

**Can providers also buy from other sellers?**
Yes! Tap **Browse & Shop** on your business dashboard to switch into the
buyer experience. You can browse categories, search, add to cart, and check
out exactly like a regular user — your purchases and your sales stay
completely separate.

**How do I earn points?**
Every successfully completed sale earns points automatically. Buyers earn
points for completed orders too. Points are shown on your dashboard.

**What is my public rating?**
Your provider profile shows your average star rating, number of completed
sales, and completion rate — all calculated from real orders, so customers
can trust the numbers they see.

## Ratings and reviews

**How do ratings work?**
After an order is completed you can rate the seller. Ratings feed the
average shown on the seller's profile, so it reflects real experiences.

## Account and security

**How do I change my password or details?**
Open your profile from the bottom menu and use the settings options there.

**What are the Terms & Privacy Policy?**
The rules every member agrees to at registration: keep contact after escrow
respectful, never move payments off the app, represent listings honestly,
and what happens if the rules are broken (warnings up to suspension). It
also explains exactly how your phone number, ID, and location data are
used. You can read the full text any time from the link on the registration
screen or in the app.

**The other party isn't responding — what do I do?**
Open **Help & Support** from your profile and tell the assistant about the
problem. It will collect the details (the provider's name, their city, your
phone number, and the order number if you have it) and submit a ticket to
our support team, who will contact you. Providers can reach support
directly at **0917686990**.

**Nobody responded to my ticket — what now?**
If your submitted issue hasn't been answered within about 10 hours, the
assistant will proactively share our direct contact details — phone
**0917686990** and email **muktarbiya329@gmail.com** — so you can reach us
directly. You can also use them at any time.

**How do I delete my account?**
Account deletion is described in the account section of your profile. If
you need help, contact support.

**Is my personal information safe?**
Your documents are stored privately and can only be seen by you and our
verification team. Your phone number and exact location are never shown to
other buyers before a payment is confirmed.

# UI strings reference (for the help assistant)

Common buttons and labels in the app:

- **Sign In / Sign Up / Get Started** — account screens
- **Cart** — top-right icon while browsing; shows the number of items
- **My Orders** — your order history and current orders
- **My Points** — your points total, on your profile
- **Help & Support** — this help center, on your profile
- **Wallet** — provider earnings and subscription status
- **Share live location** — after payment confirmation, shares your GPS
  with the provider for that order
- **Get directions** — opens turn-by-turn navigation in your maps app
- **Pending approval** — your account is awaiting review; you'll get a
  notification when it's approved
