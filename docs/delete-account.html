Delete your account

This page is public. You do not need to install the app, create an account, or sign in to read it, and it is available worldwide.

Last updated: 1 October 2026 · Applies to the 5birr (Aliob) Android app and its account database.

How to request deletion in the app

  1. Open the 5birr app and sign in to the account you want to close.
  2. Open your Profile screen.
  3. Scroll to the bottom of the account menu and tap Delete Account. It is the last item, shown in red, because it is a serious action.
  4. On the Delete Account screen, type why you are leaving in Why are you leaving? (required). Please write at least a few words — the person who reads your request is a human, not an automatic filter.
  5. If you want to add anything else, use Anything else we should know? (optional).
  6. Tap SUBMIT REQUEST.
Submitting a request does not delete anything. Your account keeps working normally — you can keep ordering, booking rides and using your wallet — until a staff member approves the request. This is deliberate: deletion is a decision with a person attached to it, and you can see who made it.

What happens next

  • The request appears on the same screen under Your requests, marked Under review. You can only have one open request at a time.
  • A staff member with the account-deletion job reads your reason and decides. They may add a note back to you.
  • You are notified inside the app either way. Approved requests show as Approved, declined requests as Declined, together with the note from our team. Declining is not the end of the road — you can submit a new request afterwards.
  • When a request is approved, the account is closed at the next maintenance pass and the data below is removed or detached.

What is deleted

When your account is closed, the following is removed from our database. Nothing here is anonymised — it is deleted.

  • Your sign-in account — the email or phone number you registered with, and the password hash.
  • Your profile — full name, phone number, email address, profile photo and the rest of your personal details.
  • Your roles and access — your customer, business, driver or staff role, and every permission attached to it.
  • Your uploaded verification documents — identity documents, business licences, driving licences and the live-location data captured with them, together with the review decision and who made it.
  • Your messages and notifications — every in-app notification, your notification preferences, your saved favourites, your likes, your cart, your chat messages, and the push tokens registered for your devices.
  • Your support appeals — the appeals you submitted, including the photo, description, phone number and email address you gave with them.
  • Your one-time codes and rate-limit records.
  • If you ran a business on 5birr (shop, service provider or driver): your business profile, product listings and their photos, your store details, your vehicles and their photos, your driver status, your subscription, your staff assignments, and your provider wallet together with its transaction history.
If you ran a business, the same request closes it. Nothing is special about a shop, a service provider or a driver: the orders, ride records and wallet entries that point at your business are detached the same way a customer's are, so your listings, gallery, vehicles and wallet go while the accounting history stays. You do not need to contact us separately to close a business account.
About the files behind your documents and photos. The database records for your documents, product photos, gallery photos and vehicle photos are deleted as part of the closure. The underlying image files sit in our private storage, where nothing references them any more; they are purged from storage within 30 days of the closure date. They are never re-shown or re-used.

What is kept, and why

A few records have to survive the account, because they are about money that has already moved or about the platform's own audit trail rather than about you. These records are detached: the link to your account is set to nothing, so they can no longer be reached from your account, are not shown to any other user, and cannot be traced back to a login. Your profile — the row that held your name, phone number, email address and photo — is one of the rows deleted above.

Some of those records keep a copy of a detail as plain text. An order keeps the delivery address you typed into it; a ride keeps the pickup and destination you entered; a support ticket keeps the phone number you typed into it; an internal staff log can keep the name of the staff member who acted. Those copies stay on the record they belong to, because the record stays. If you want a particular record corrected or removed, email us with the order, ride or ticket reference and we will deal with that row.
KeptWhy
Orders, order status history, payment agreements and payment proof records Accounting and tax records for money that has already changed hands. The delivery address you typed on the order stays as part of the order record, because it is what the order was fulfilled to, and the link to the business it was placed with is removed along with the account.
Ride requests, ride status history and ratings The same accounting reason, plus safety: the pickup and destination points and text of a completed ride are part of the trip record. The rating you left and the rating you received are kept as statements about those trips.
Support tickets and the phone number you gave on them Our obligation to be able to show what was reported and how it was resolved. The reporter_phone you typed into a ticket is kept on that ticket.
Audit logs, admin actions, approval history and administrative history The platform's own compliance record of who approved or changed what. The acting account is set to nothing.
Points balances and points ledger entries Financial-style balances are kept as detached balances with no account behind them.
Public catalogue data: categories, fee settings, public provider listings and trust scores, staff job definitions, system settings None of this is personal to you. It is the public catalogue and configuration the app runs on, and it is the same for everybody.

Nothing on this list can be used to contact you, and none of it is sold or shared for advertising. If you want the exact rows we hold for you before deciding, ask us and we will provide them.

Depends on whether you also ran a business

One table is deleted for a business account and kept for a customer account, because it hangs off your provider wallet when you have one.

  • A wallet recharge request is deleted if it belonged to the provider wallet you ran, together with that wallet. If you were only ever a customer, it is kept for accounting with the approving account set to nothing.
  • Staff action log: deleted if the log line belongs to your own staff record, which goes with the account. If you merely acted in someone else's log, the line is kept with the actor link set to nothing, and the actor name written on that line stays as text.
  • Staff job assignments: deleted with your own staff record. Assignments you granted or revoked on somebody else are kept as the record of who changed what, with the link set to nothing and the name written on the line kept as text.
  • Staff warnings: deleted with your own staff record. Warnings you issued to somebody else are kept, with the link set to nothing and the name written on the line kept as text.
  • Staff task completions: deleted with your own staff record. Completions recorded against somebody else are kept, with the link set to nothing.
  • Referral codes: deleted with your own staff record. A code you issued that belongs to another staff member is kept, with your account link set to nothing.
