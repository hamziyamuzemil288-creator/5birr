# Privacy Policy for 5birr

**Last updated:** September 2026

5birr ("we", "our", "the app") is a marketplace application that connects shops, service providers, and vehicles for rides and delivery. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices regarding that information.

## Information We Collect

**Account Information**
When you register, we collect your name, phone number, email address, and password (stored securely, never in plain text).

**Location Information**
We collect precise location data when you use ride-booking, delivery-tracking, or nearby-shop features. Location is used to match you with nearby drivers, providers, and shops, and to enable real-time tracking during active rides or deliveries. You can disable location access in your device settings, though this will limit core app functionality.

**Wallet & Payment Information**
We collect transaction history, wallet balance, and payment details necessary to process top-ups, payments, and withdrawals. We do not store full card numbers; payment processing is handled through secure third-party payment providers.

**Provider & Vehicle Information**
If you register as a shop, service provider, or driver, we collect additional information such as business details, vehicle information, license/verification documents, and service listings.

**Usage Information**
We collect information about how you use the app — orders placed, rides taken, services booked, and app interactions — to improve our services and provide customer support.

**Device Information**
We collect device type, operating system, and push notification tokens to deliver order updates, ride status, and account notifications.

## How We Use Your Information

- To provide and operate core app features (marketplace browsing, ride booking, delivery tracking, wallet management)
- To match users with nearby shops, providers, drivers
- To process payments and maintain transaction records
- To send notifications about orders, rides, and account activity
- To verify provider and driver identities for platform safety
- To improve app performance and troubleshoot issues
- To comply with legal obligations

## Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with:
- **Service providers and drivers** you interact with, to fulfill your orders/rides (e.g., your location and contact details are shared with your assigned driver)
- **Payment processors**, to complete transactions
- **Law enforcement or regulators**, if required by law

## Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active, or as needed to provide services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. Transaction, accounting and audit records are kept in a form that is no longer linked to your account; `delete-account.md` lists exactly what is deleted and what is kept.

## Your Rights

You may:
- Access, update, or correct your account information within the app
- Request deletion of your account and associated data, from within the app
  (Profile > Delete Account) or by email. See [delete-account.md](delete-account.md)
  for the full flow and for exactly what is deleted and what is kept.
- Disable location permissions at any time via device settings
- Opt out of non-essential push notifications in app settings

## Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures, including encrypted data transmission and secure authentication, to protect your information. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

## Children's Privacy

5birr is not intended for use by children under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
